# Major Arcana

## 1000 Eye Circlet

- **Tier:** major
- **Palette:** Delicate Gold — `#D49B41`
- **Palette:** Obsidian Veil — `#191C1E`
- **Palette:** Purple Iris — `#694092`
- **Touchstones:** scout—eyes—reveal
- **Sensories (swirl):** blinking and looking eyes, wet organic matter
- **Sensories (hand):** vision shatters like glass, bobbing eye
- **Activation:** plucking the eye from the center.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/magic/control/hypnosis-mesmerism-eye.webp

You can mentally control a floating magical eye (4d power pool), seeing what it sees. If destroyed, make a defense roll against rattled.
