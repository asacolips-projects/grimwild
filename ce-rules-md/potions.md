# Potions

## Jar of Mischief

- **Tier:** trivial
- **Palette:** Wildflower Pink — `#D64582`
- **Palette:** Sly Purple — `#782E62`
- **Palette:** Joyous Teal — `#1BC2B3`
- **Touchstones:** chaos—random—unknown function
- **Sensories (swirl):** pink vapors, sparks fly, strong berry scent
- **Sensories (hand):** butterfly flutters inside, loud pop as lid comes off
- **Activation:** wrenching the heavy seal off.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/consumables/potions/potion-jar-corked-labeled-purple-pink.webp

Opening this jar causes immediate chaos, as a wild magic surge (3d story roll). The function depends on the wild magic.

## Aetheric Elixir

- **Tier:** major
- **Palette:** Phantom Mist — `#D1E8E2`
- **Palette:** Ectoplasm — `#4AA382`
- **Palette:** Shadowy Gray — `#263238`
- **Touchstones:** ghost—freedom—transform
- **Sensories (swirl):** freezing cold, swirling ectoplasm
- **Sensories (hand):** vial drops as it passes through your hand
- **Activation:** drinking it, and exhaling a held breath.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/consumables/potions/bottle-conical-fumes-green.webp

- **Palette:** Sinuous Muscle — `#6F4B4F`
- **Palette:** Fibrous Flesh — `#D99E91`
- **Palette:** Soul Lavender — `#776DC2`
- **Touchstones:** life—replicate—summon
- **Sensories (swirl):** soft flesh-like texture, unnervingly heavy, warm
- **Sensories (hand):** splits like egg, missing memories of last year

## Bottled Wish

- **Tier:** mythic
- **Palette:** Pure Gold — `#F2D06B`
- **Palette:** Celestial Blue — `#1F3A60`
- **Palette:** Eternal White — `#FFFFFF`
- **Touchstones:** single—wish—granted
- **Sensories (swirl):** hypnotic cosmic swirl, unintelligible whispers
- **Sensories (hand):** glow beneath skin, sense of being overwhelmed
- **Activation:** closing your eyes and making your wish.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/consumables/potions/potion-bottle-corked-fancy-orange.webp

The drinker of this world-altering elixir is granted a single wish. Trying to gain more wishes, directly or indirectly, invites the magic to twist your intent...
