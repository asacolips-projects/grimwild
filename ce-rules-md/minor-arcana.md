# Minor Arcana

## Loyal Spear

- **Tier:** minor
- **Palette:** Weathered Ash — `#8B7E74`
- **Palette:** Honor Brass — `#C5A059`
- **Palette:** Bloodied Steel — `#5C2E2E`
- **Touchstones:** fling—return—summon
- **Sensories (swirl):** polished shine, dried blood
- **Sensories (hand):** white knuckled grip, acts with owner as one
- **Activation:** giving a sharp whistle after thrown.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/weapons/polearms/spear-simple-hooked.webp

After being thrown, this spear can be recalled. It disappears in a puff of arcane mist and reappears in your hand.

## Magebane Shield

- **Tier:** minor
- **Palette:** COLD IRON — `#3A3F47`
- **Palette:** NULL SILVER — `#A3A9B3`
- **Palette:** ARCANE VIOLET — `#6A4E85`
- **Touchstones:** anti-magic—sense—protect
- **Sensories (swirl):** violet fractures, faint purple pulse, magic buzz
- **Sensories (hand):** numb fingers, static raised hair
- **Activation:** tightening your grip.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/equipment/shield/buckler-wooden-boss-glowing-blue.webp

This shield faintly glows in the presence of magic, and can stop magic just as it well as it can stop mundane weapons.

## Poisonheart Choker

- **Palette:** NIGHTSHADE — `#3B1F34`
- **Palette:** BRUISED BLACK — `#1C141A`
- **Touchstones:** acid—blood—attack
- **Sensories (swirl):** acrid smell masked by lavender and rose
- **Sensories (hand):** subtle tightening around throat, acid dripping

## Starlight Flask

- **Tier:** minor
- **Palette:** COSMIC INDIGO — `#1B1F38`
- **Palette:** SILVER GLINT — `#D1D7E0`
- **Palette:** SHINING LIGHT — `#EEDDA3`
- **Touchstones:** light—curious—summon
- **Sensories (swirl):** swirling mist, burst of light, shadows scatter
- **Sensories (hand):** mote hops to get your attention, sudden vertigo
- **Activation:** shaking and uncorking the flask.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/consumables/potions/potion-bottle-fumes-blue.webp

Once per session, you can unstopper this flask to have motes of light scatter, illuminating the nearby areas.

## Whisperknife

- **Tier:** minor
- **Palette:** NIGHT BLACK — `#0D1117`
- **Palette:** MURKY GRAY — `#333842`
- **Palette:** ENCHANTED STEEL — `#4E5D6C`
- **Touchstones:** silence—voice—hinder
- **Sensories (swirl):** absorbs light, tightening shadow
- **Sensories (hand):** faint echoes of the dead in your mind
- **Activation:** being plunged into flesh.
- **Suggested icon:** /icons/weapons/daggers/dagger-curved-black.webp

When this knife is in flesh, the victim can't speak or otherwise make noise. *No more last words.*
